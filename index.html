Questions

Good to know

When can I get it?

Ormy is coming to iPhone soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the day it's on the App Store.

Is it only for people with ADHD?

No. It's built by someone with ADHD, for anyone whose brain drops the important stuff.

Does it use AI?

Only if you switch it on. When it's on, Ormy's AI sees a reminder's title and how far away it is. Nothing else.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Ormy is iPhone only for now.
