How to remember birthdays with ADHD

Why birthdays slip when you have ADHD, even for people you love, and what actually helps, including the free setting already on your iPhone. Sourced, from someone with ADHD.

By Kyle Ormesher, founder of OrmyUpdated 1 November 2026 · 6 min read

The short answer

Stop relying on your head, and stop relying on the day itself. Put every birthday in one place once, make it repeat each year, and get warned weeks before, not just on the morning. By the day, it's too late to buy the card. Then name each warning after the job, like "Buy Mum's card", so you know what to do when it arrives.

I built Ormy, a reminder app, so I'm not neutral. I have ADHD, but I'm not a doctor. This is what the sources below say, not medical advice. If something here is wrong, email me and I'll fix it.

Why do I keep forgetting birthdays with ADHD?

Most people don't forget that their mum has a birthday. They know the date, or roughly. It just doesn't come to mind in time to do anything about it. The research points to two reasons.

Working memory is weaker. It's your brain's sticky note. A review of 38 studies found that adults with ADHD do worse on working memory tests, on average. A birthday three weeks away has nothing holding it in your head.

Remembering at the right moment is harder. In one small study, adults with ADHD were much worse at remembering to do something at a set time. When the task was tied to something happening in front of them, they did no worse than anyone else. A date on a calendar is the first kind. Nothing around you says "it's nearly the 14th". So don't read too much into one study, but it fits.

Forgetting a date doesn't mean you care less. Caring just doesn't put the date in front of you at the right moment.

Why is a reminder on the day too late?

Because the birthday isn't really the job. The job is the card, the present, the post or the booking, and those all have to happen before the day.

A reminder at 9am on the day leaves no time to buy or post anything. Then there's the other catch: a normal notification goes off once. Swipe it away while you're busy, and it's gone. I compared the apps that keep going in persistent reminder apps.

How do you remember birthdays with ADHD?

ADHD researcher Russell Barkley says help has to be at the "point of performance": the place and time you need to act. Everything below is a version of that.

  1. Put them all in one place, once. Don't wait to add them as they come up, because you won't. Take ten minutes with your phone and ask a family member for the dates you don't know.
  2. Make each one repeat every year. If it's a one-off, you've only fixed this year.
  3. Get warned weeks before, not just on the day. If you post anything, try two weeks, one week, three days and the day before. If you only ever send a text, a couple of warnings will do.
  4. Name it after the job. "Buy Mum's card" tells you what to do. "Mum's birthday" only tells you what's happening, and it's easy to swipe away a fact.
  5. Tie the job to a moment. "When I'm next in the supermarket, I'll buy the card." Psychologists call these if-then plans. A review of 36 comparisons found they help people remember to do things later. (Those studies weren't in people with ADHD, so it isn't proven for us. But it's free to try.)
  6. Keep a few cards and stamps at home. Buy five at once and put them where you'll see them. Next time, the job takes two minutes and needs no trip to the shop.
  7. Write the message when you remember it. On iPhone, you can schedule an iMessage up to 14 days ahead. In the chat, tap the plus button, then Send Later. A warning a week before can become a message that sends itself on the day.

Some people get on better with a wall calendar by the door than with a phone. Try both and keep what you actually use.

How do you get birthday reminders on iPhone?

There's a free version already on your phone:

  1. Add the birthday to the person's contact.
  2. In the Calendar app, open your list of calendars and tap Birthdays. That shows birthdays from your contacts alongside your events.
  3. Go to Settings, then Apps, then Calendar, then Default Alert Times. Tap Birthdays and choose when you want the alert.

It's a good place to start, and it might be all you need. The limit is that you choose one option, and it applies to every birthday. If you want several warnings, or a different plan for the people you post things to, you'll need reminders of your own or a separate app.

What if I've already forgotten?

Send something today. A late message beats no message, and a short one beats an essay about why you forgot. Something like:

"Late, but I mean it: happy birthday. Coffee on me this week?"

Then set next year's reminder while you've got your phone in your hand. That's the only part that changes anything.

Could it be something else?

Yes. The NHS says memory problems can also come from things that are common and treatable, like stress, anxiety, depression and sleep problems. It says to see a GP if memory problems are affecting your day-to-day life. There's more on this in ADHD forgetfulness.

Questions people ask

Is forgetting birthdays a sign of ADHD? Not on its own. Anyone can forget a birthday. The NHS does list being forgetful among the signs of ADHD in adults, so if it's part of a bigger pattern, it's worth talking to a GP.

Why do I forget the birthdays of people I love? Because remembering at the right moment is a memory job, and how much you care doesn't change it. Nothing around you says the date is coming, so it doesn't come to mind until it's too late.

How far ahead should a birthday reminder go off? Far enough to do the job. If you post a card or a present, start at two weeks. If you only send a message, a few days and the morning will do.

Should I name the reminder after the person or the job? The job. "Buy Sam's present" gets done more easily than "Sam's birthday", because you can see what to do next.

Can I schedule a birthday text on iPhone? Yes, if it's an iMessage. You can schedule it up to 14 days ahead with Send Later, and you can edit or delete it before it goes.

What's the best app for remembering birthdays? One that comes to you and warns you early, because you won't remember to open anything else. The iPhone's Calendar is free, so try that first. I compared the main reminder apps in the best ADHD reminder apps.
